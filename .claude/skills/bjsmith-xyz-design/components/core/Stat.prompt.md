Trip/collection counters as they appear on `/travel/` — a hairline grid of panels, each with a 1.5rem tabular figure and an uppercase 11px caption.

```jsx
<StatGrid columns={3}>
  <Stat value="184" label="days on the road" />
  <Stat value="27" label="stops" />
  <Stat value="9" label="countries" />
</StatGrid>
```
